## D0 suite composition (seeds 1–20)

Generated by `python tools/gen_suite.py`; per-seed attacks are listed in `data/d0/suite.json`. Every seed has A1, A2 and decoy D1; A3 is in 12 of 20 seeds, D2 in 15, D3 in 11. See docs/wp/EV-2.md for what varies.
